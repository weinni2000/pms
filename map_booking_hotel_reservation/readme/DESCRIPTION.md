This module shows the map of map_booking on the "Reservation Line" page of a
hotel reservation. The zones are colored for the stay of the reservation: a
zone is free while one of its rooms is free from check-in to check-out, and
occupied once all of them are booked by a confirmed reservation or a folio.

Clicking a free zone picks its room and fills in the reservation lines: the
room is added to the line of its room type, or to a new line when the
reservation has none for that type yet.
