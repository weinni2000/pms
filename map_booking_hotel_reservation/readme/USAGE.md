1. Open or create a hotel reservation and set its booking dates.
2. On the "Reservation Line" page, choose the map. A new reservation starts on
   the first map with hotel rooms on it. The map follows the rooms of the
   reservation: when none of them is on the chosen map, the map holding most
   of them is opened.
3. Click a green (free) zone: its room is added to the reservation lines, and
   the zone is highlighted. Click a zone holding several rooms again to add the
   next free one. Clicking a highlighted zone with no free room left removes
   its rooms from the reservation again.
4. Each zone shows how many guests its rooms hold. Under the map, the line
   "Selected rooms" counts the rooms of the reservation, and "Guests" compares
   what they hold with the adults and children of the reservation: it turns
   green once there is room for everybody.
5. Remove a picked room with the cross on its chip under the map, or delete the
   line in the list; both keep the map and the lines in step.

The map can only be changed while the reservation is a draft. A confirmed
reservation books its rooms, so they show as occupied on its own map.
