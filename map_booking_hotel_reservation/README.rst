This module shows the map of map_booking on the "Reservation Line" page
of a hotel reservation. The zones are colored for the stay of the
reservation: a zone is free while one of its rooms is free from check-in
to check-out, and occupied once all of them are booked by a confirmed
reservation or a folio.

Clicking a free zone picks its room and fills in the reservation lines:
the room is added to the line of its room type, or to a new line when
the reservation has none for that type yet.

Configuration
=============

Draw the zones of the map and link them to hotel rooms, or room types,
as described in the map_booking_hotel_bridge module.

Usage
=====

1. Open or create a hotel reservation and set its booking dates.
2. On the "Reservation Line" page, choose the map. A new reservation
   starts on the first map with hotel rooms on it. The map follows the
   rooms of the reservation: when none of them is on the chosen map, the
   map holding most of them is opened.
3. Click a green (free) zone: its room is added to the reservation
   lines, and the zone is highlighted. Click a zone holding several
   rooms again to add the next free one. Clicking a highlighted zone
   with no free room left removes its rooms from the reservation again.
4. Each zone shows how many guests its rooms hold. Under the map, the
   line "Selected rooms" counts the rooms of the reservation, and
   "Guests" compares what they hold with the adults and children of the
   reservation: it turns green once there is room for everybody.
5. Remove a picked room with the cross on its chip under the map, or
   delete the line in the list; both keep the map and the lines in step.

The map can only be changed while the reservation is a draft. A
confirmed reservation books its rooms, so they show as occupied on its
own map.
