Colors the zones of a ``map_booking`` map from the bookings of the
resources they hold, and files the map screens under the Resource
Bookings application.

Each zone lists ``resource.resource`` records. Every
``resource.booking`` that is not canceled blocks its resources for its
whole start/stop range, and a zone is free as soon as one of its
resources is free.

Configuration
=============

1. Install this module; the maps and their availability states move to
   **Resource Bookings > Maps**.
2. On a zone form, under **Resources**, pick the resources the zone
   holds. Use **Resource Bookings > Maps > Maps > Create Zone for Each
   Resource** to generate one zone per material resource that has no
   zone yet.
3. Resource Booking managers are granted the Map Booking manager rights,
   so they can edit maps, zones and availability states.

Usage
=====

Availability rules
------------------

- A booking blocks its resources from its start until its end; canceled
  bookings are ignored.
- A zone holding several resources stays free while at least one of them
  is free, and only turns occupied when all of them are booked.
- The check-out day itself is not a booked night: a stay starting on the
  day a booking ends is still free.
