Shows the ``map_booking`` map of a property in its website booking
dialog (``pms_website_sale``), at the top, above the check-in and
check-out dates.

The booked property is highlighted on the map, and the zones are colored
free or occupied for the dates entered in the dialog. Clicking another
free zone opens the booking dialog of its property instead, keeping the
dates.

Configuration
=============

No setting picks the map: the dialog shows the first map (in the order
of the maps) with a zone holding the property, either directly under
**Properties** or through one of its **Property Tags** (see
``map_booking_pms_bridge``). A property on no map gets the booking
dialog without a map.

Only published properties of the current website can be switched to from
the map; the others are drawn but cannot be picked.

Usage
=====

1. Open a property on the website and click **Book Now**.
2. Enter the check-in and check-out dates: the map colors each zone free
   or occupied for that stay, and the booked property is highlighted.
3. Click another free zone to book its property instead: its page opens
   with the booking dialog and the dates already filled in.
