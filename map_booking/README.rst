Turns a plan or photo of your site into an interactive map of its
bookable resources.

Each map is an image with clickable zones drawn on it. A zone lists the
resources it contains (``resource.resource``) and colors itself from the
real bookings of those resources, so a receptionist or a visitor sees at
a glance which pitches, rooms or units are **free** and which are
**occupied** for the dates they are checking.

What books a resource comes from a separate bridge module:
``map_booking_resource_booking_bridge`` colors the zones from Resource
Booking reservations, ``map_booking_pms_bridge`` colors them from PMS
reservations. Without one of them the zones carry no availability
information and are drawn in the neutral color.

The map widget itself (rendering, zooming labels, legend, polygon
editor) is provided by the ``map_widget`` module; this module adds the
records, the availability logic and the backend screens.

Configuration
=============

1. Install one of the bridge modules to get availability on the maps:
   ``map_booking_resource_booking_bridge`` (Resource Booking) or
   ``map_booking_pms_bridge`` (PMS). It also files the map screens under
   its own application menu.
2. Go to **Maps > Maps** and create a map.
3. Upload the plan or photo of your site as the map image.
4. On the **Zones** tab, use the bridge's **Create Zone for Each
   Resource** or **Create Zone for Each Property** button to generate
   one zone per bookable unit that has no zone yet, or add the zones by
   hand.
5. On the **Map Preview** tab, pick a zone, click **New polygon** and
   click around its boundary on the image; click the first point again
   to save it. Then drag, add (click an edge) or remove (double-click)
   corners of an existing outline, or drag the whole outline with the
   left mouse button, whenever the layout changes. Outlines always stay
   inside the image.
6. Restrict which units each zone holds on the zone form, under
   **Resources** or **Properties** (shown by the matching bridge
   module).
7. The labels and colors of the availability states are editable records
   under **Maps > Availability States**.

Usage
=====

Check the availability of a map
-------------------------------

1. Open a map and go to its **Availability** tab.
2. Pick the stay period. Every zone is colored from the bookings
   overlapping those nights: the check-out day itself is not a booked
   night.
3. Toggle **Refresh** to recompute the colors, e.g. after a booking was
   made in another window.

Availability rules
------------------

- A booking blocks its resources from its start until its end; canceled
  bookings are ignored.
- A zone holding several resources stays free while at least one of them
  is free, and only turns occupied when all of them are booked.
- A zone without resources, or a database without a bridge module,
  carries no availability information and is drawn in the neutral color.
- The **Free** and **Occupied** states, and any state you add
  (maintenance, closed, ...), are records: rename them, recolor them, or
  translate them without touching the code.

Reuse the same map data elsewhere
---------------------------------

``map.booking.map`` and ``map.booking.zone`` expose the data the map
needs as plain dictionaries, ready for the ``map_widget`` component:

- ``map.booking.zone._get_map_shapes(state, colors)`` - one zone shape;
- ``map.booking.map.preview_data_calculated`` - image, zones and legend;
- ``map.booking.map.test_preview_data_calculated`` - the same, colored
  for the checked stay period.

Override ``_get_bookable_units()`` on ``map.booking.zone`` to plug in
another booking engine: append ``(unit, is_busy)`` pairs to the result
of the parent method, so several engines can coexist on the same map.
