1. Install one of the bridge modules to get availability on the maps:
   ``map_booking_resource_booking_bridge`` (Resource Booking) or
   ``map_booking_pms_bridge`` (PMS). It also files the map screens under its own
   application menu.
2. Go to **Maps > Maps** and create a map.
3. Upload the plan or photo of your site as the map image.
4. On the **Zones** tab, use the bridge's **Create Zone for Each Resource** or
   **Create Zone for Each Property** button to generate one zone per bookable
   unit that has no zone yet, or add the zones by hand.
5. On the **Map Preview** tab, pick a zone, click **New polygon** and click
   around its boundary on the image; click the first point again to save it.
   Then drag, add (click an edge) or
   remove (double-click) corners of an existing outline, or drag the whole
   outline with the left mouse button, whenever the layout changes. Outlines
   always stay inside the image.
6. Restrict which units each zone holds on the zone form, under **Resources**
   or **Properties** (shown by the matching bridge module).
7. The labels and colors of the availability states are editable records under
   **Maps > Availability States**.
