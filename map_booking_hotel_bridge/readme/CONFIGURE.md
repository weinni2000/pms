1. Install this module; the maps and their availability states move to
   **Hotel Management > Maps**.
2. On a zone form, under **Rooms**, pick the rooms the zone holds, or pick
   **Room Types** to hold every room of one of them. Use
   **Hotel Management > Maps > Maps > Create Zone for Each Room** to generate
   one zone per room that has no zone yet.
3. Hotel managers are granted the Map Booking manager rights, so they can edit
   maps, zones and availability states.
