Colors the zones of a ``map_booking`` map from the reservations and
folios of the hotel rooms they hold (OCA ``vertical-hotel``), and files
the map screens under the Hotel Management application.

Each zone lists ``hotel.room`` records, picked one by one or through
their ``hotel.room.type``. Every confirmed ``hotel.reservation`` and
every ``hotel.folio`` that is not canceled blocks its rooms for its
whole check-in/check-out range, and a zone is free as soon as one of its
rooms is free.

Configuration
=============

1. Install this module; the maps and their availability states move to
   **Hotel Management > Maps**.
2. On a zone form, under **Rooms**, pick the rooms the zone holds, or
   pick **Room Types** to hold every room of one of them. Use **Hotel
   Management > Maps > Maps > Create Zone for Each Room** to generate
   one zone per room that has no zone yet.
3. Hotel managers are granted the Map Booking manager rights, so they
   can edit maps, zones and availability states.

Usage
=====

Availability rules
------------------

- A confirmed (or done) reservation blocks its rooms from check-in until
  check-out; draft and canceled reservations are ignored.
- A folio blocks its rooms unless it is canceled.
- A zone holding several rooms stays free while at least one of them is
  free, and only turns occupied when all of them are booked.
- The check-out day itself is not a booked night: a stay starting on the
  day a reservation ends is still free.

Demo data
---------

The "Holiday Home" demo map holds two flats and a dormitory whose six
beds are rooms of the "Dormitory Bed" type. Flat 1 and three of the beds
are booked today, so Flat 1 shows occupied while Flat 2 and the
dormitory stay free.
