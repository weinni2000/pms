Colors the zones of a ``map_booking`` map from the PMS reservations of
the properties they hold, and files the map screens under the Properties
application.

Each zone lists ``pms.property`` records, picked one by one or through
their ``pms.tag`` tags. Every ``pms.reservation`` that is booked blocks
its property for its whole start/stop range, and a zone is free as soon
as one of its properties is free.

Configuration
=============

1. Install this module; the maps and their availability states move to
   **Properties > Maps**.
2. On a zone form, under **Properties**, pick the properties the zone
   holds, or pick **Property Tags** to hold every property carrying one
   of them. Use **Properties > Maps > Maps > Create Zone for Each
   Property** to generate one zone per property that has no zone yet.
3. PMS managers are granted the Map Booking manager rights, so they can
   edit maps, zones and availability states.

Usage
=====

Availability rules
------------------

- A reservation blocks its property from check-in until check-out; draft
  (``New``) and canceled reservations are ignored.
- A zone holding several properties stays free while at least one of
  them is free, and only turns occupied when all of them are booked.
- The check-out day itself is not a booked night: a stay starting on the
  day a reservation ends is still free.
